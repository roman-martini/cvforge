# Staff Backend Engineer — Contoso Payments Inc.

**Remote (Americas) · Full-time · Seattle, WA HQ**

Contoso Payments builds the settlement rails behind roughly 8,000 online
merchants across North and South America. We move money, so the boring
properties matter more than the exciting ones: correctness, latency you can put
in a contract, and a pager that stays quiet.

We are hiring a Staff Backend Engineer to own the authorization path end to end.

## What you'll do

- Own the design and operation of our authorization and settlement services.
- Set and defend service level objectives; carry the pager for what you build.
- Bring the rest of the team along: reviews, runbooks, and fewer heroics.

## What we're looking for

- 7+ years of backend engineering experience, with at least 3 at a senior level.
- Production Go. We are a Go shop and are not looking to change that.
- Kubernetes in production, including debugging it at 3 a.m.
- Experience designing and operating gRPC (or equivalent RPC) services.
- PostgreSQL at scale: partitioning, query plans, replication.
- Professional written English — most of our decisions live in documents.

## Nice to have

- Payments, ledgers or regulated fintech background.
- Terraform or another infrastructure-as-code toolchain.
- A track record of mentoring engineers.

## Logistics

- Fully remote within the Americas, with at least four hours of daily overlap
  with 9:00–18:00 America/Los_Angeles.
- We contract internationally through a local employer of record. **We do not
  sponsor US work visas for this role.**
- A currently valid Kubernetes administration certification is required for
  access to the production cluster.
- Target start: within 45 days of offer.
